# SignCadence
### AI-Powered Assistive Communication Technology

## 1. Project Overview

SignCadence is an AI-powered assistive communication solution designed to bridge the communication gap between Deaf people and individuals who do not understand sign language.

The project aims to develop a device that translates sign language into spoken words and converts spoken language into written text, enabling more accessible two-way communication.

The solution is intended for use in schools, hospitals, workplaces, public institutions and everyday social interactions in Namibia and beyond.

## 2. Problem Statement

According to the World Federation of the Deaf, more than 70 million Deaf people live worldwide, with over 80% living in developing countries. Collectively, Deaf communities use more than 300 different sign languages.

People who use sign language frequently experience communication barriers when interacting with individuals who do not understand sign language, particularly where professional interpreters are unavailable.

These barriers can limit their participation in education, employment, healthcare and social activities.

There is a need for accessible assistive technology that supports communication and promotes social inclusion.

## 3. Proposed Solution

SignCadence proposes an AI-powered assistive communication device with two main functionalities:

- **Sign Language to Speech:** Uses AI-based sign recognition to interpret signs and translate them into spoken words.
- **Speech to Text:** Converts spoken communication into written text that can be read by a person who uses sign language.

## 4. Project Objectives

1. Develop an AI-powered sign language recognition system.
2. Translate recognised signs into spoken language.
3. Convert spoken language into readable text.
4. Improve communication accessibility in public and private institutions.
5. Promote independence and social inclusion.
6. Involve Deaf communities in the design, development and evaluation of the solution.

## 5. Target Users

- Deaf and hard-of-hearing people.
- Students and educators.
- Healthcare professionals.
- Employers and employees.
- Public institutions.
- People who interact with sign language users.

## 6. Potential Technologies

The technologies to be explored include:

- Python
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Computer Vision
- OpenCV
- MediaPipe
- Speech Recognition
- Text-to-Speech technology

## 7. Guiding Principle

**Nothing About Us Without Us.**

SignCadence recognises the importance of involving Deaf people and their communities throughout the design, development and testing process to ensure that the technology responds to their actual communication needs.

## 8. Project Status

Currently in the initial development and research stage.

## 9. Project Vision

To contribute to a more inclusive society where communication barriers do not prevent Deaf people from accessing education, healthcare, employment and social opportunities.
